# Project Overview

MediMind AI is a modern HealthTech platform designed to help patients and healthcare professionals manage medical information securely and efficiently.

The platform provides appointment scheduling, medical record management, tracking, medicine reminders, and role-based dashboards for patients, doctors, and administrators.

The long-term vision is to integrate Artificial Intelligence to assist users in understanding medical reports, explaining prescriptions, provide health insights, and improving healthcare accessibility while keeping healthcare professionals in control of clinical decisions.

---

# Problem Statement

Many healthcare systems rely on disconnected applications or paper-based workflows, making it difficult for patients to access their medical records and for healthcare providers to efficiently manage appointments and patient information.

Patients often struggle to:

- Keep all medical records in one place
- Remember medication schedules
- Track appointments
- Understand complex medical reports

Healthcare professionals often require a centralized system to manage patient information securely while maintaining efficiency and data integrity.

---

# Proposed Solution

MediMind AI provides a secure, centralized healthcare management platform that enables:

- Secure authentication and role-based access.
- Appointment scheduling
- Digital medical records
- Prescription management
- Medicine reminders
- Notification system
- AI-powered healthcare assistance(future)

# Out of Scope (Version 1)

The following features are intentionally excluded from Version 1:

- AI Chat Assistant
- OCR
- Video Consultation
- Online payments
- Multi-hospital support
- Wearable Device Integration
- Health Risk Prediction
- Voice Assistant

These features are planned for future releases.
